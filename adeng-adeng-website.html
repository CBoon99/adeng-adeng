The Best Spot in Indonesia

Swim with Wild Sea Turtles Right Here

Adeng-Adeng is located on Gili Meno's famous turtle coast, just steps from the Turtle Sanctuary and the island's best snorkeling spot. This is one of the only places on Earth where you're virtually guaranteed to swim with wild sea turtles.

🐢 Seriously — you can't move for turtles here! Step into the water right in front of our restaurant and you'll be swimming alongside green sea turtles and hawksbill turtles within minutes.

The Gili Islands are home to an incredible population of endangered sea turtles, protected by local conservation efforts. Gili Meno, the quietest of the three Gilis, offers the most peaceful and uncrowded turtle encounters — no boats, no crowds, just you and these magnificent creatures.

Why Gili Meno?

The Quiet Island Paradise

Gili Meno is the smallest and most peaceful of the three Gili Islands, located off the northwest coast of Lombok, Indonesia. No cars, no motorbikes — just white sand beaches, crystal-clear waters, and the slow rhythm of island life.

While Gili Trawangan (Gili T) is known for parties and Gili Air for its mix of vibes, Gili Meno is where you come to truly disconnect. Honeymooners, nature lovers, and anyone seeking tranquility will find their perfect escape here.

Island Life

A Glimpse of Paradise

Explore the Area

Nearby Attractions

From the turtle sanctuary next door to the majestic volcano across the water — adventure awaits.

🚶 2 min walk

Turtle Sanctuary

The famous Gili Meno Turtle Sanctuary is right next door! Visit the conservation center, see baby turtles, and learn about protecting these endangered species. Free entry, donations appreciated.

🤿 10 min swim

The NEST Underwater Statues

Swim or dive to see Jason deCaires Taylor's incredible underwater sculpture installation. 48 life-size figures in a circle on the seabed — a truly unique experience.

🚤 45 min to Lombok

Mount Rinjani

Indonesia's second-highest volcano is visible from Gili Meno on clear days. Take a boat to Lombok and trek to the summit for unforgettable views of the crater lake.

Island Activities

Things To Do on Gili Meno

From swimming with turtles to exploring by horseback — Gili Meno offers the perfect mix of adventure and relaxation.

Your image: snorkeling

Snorkeling & Swimming

Crystal-clear waters, vibrant coral reefs, and sea turtles everywhere. The best snorkeling is right off the beach — no boat needed. Rent gear from local shops or bring your own.

Your image: diving

Scuba Diving

World-class dive sites just minutes from shore. See turtles, reef sharks, octopus, and incredible coral formations. PADI courses available for beginners through to advanced.

Your image: horse tour

Horse & Carriage Tours

Explore the island the traditional way — by cidomo (horse-drawn carriage) or on horseback along the beach. Sunset rides are magical and support local families.

Your image: kayaking

Kayaking & SUP

Paddle across the calm turquoise waters, explore the coastline, or head out at sunrise for the most peaceful experience. Rentals available around the island.

Your image: beach relaxation

Beach & Relaxation

Sometimes the best activity is no activity at all. Find a quiet stretch of white sand, swing in a hammock, read a book, and let the island slow you down.

Your image: cycling

Island Walks & Cycling

Walk or cycle around the entire island in under two hours. No cars, no traffic — just sandy paths, coconut palms, and ocean views. Bikes available to rent.

Your image: sunset

Sunset Watching

The west coast of Gili Meno has some of Indonesia's most spectacular sunsets. Grab a cold Bintang at Adeng-Adeng and watch the sky turn orange and pink over Bali.

Your image: island hopping

Island Hopping

Visit Gili Trawangan for the nightlife or Gili Air for cafes and yoga. Regular boats connect all three islands — perfect for a day trip adventure.

Getting Around

The Cidomo: Gili Meno's Eco-Friendly Transport

On the Gili Islands, there are no cars, no motorbikes, no engines at all. The only way to get around (besides walking or cycling) is by cidomo — the traditional horse-drawn carriage that's been part of island life for generations.

These beautiful ponies are the heartbeat of local transport, and riding in a cidomo is one of the most charming ways to experience Gili Meno. The gentle clip-clop of hooves on sandy paths, the ocean breeze, the slow pace of island time — it's transport as it should be.

🌿 Eco-Friendly

Zero emissions, zero pollution. The Gilis have stayed car-free since the beginning, keeping the air clean and the beaches pristine.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Supports Families

Each cidomo supports a local family. Your ride directly contributes to the island's economy and traditional way of life.

🐴 Happy Horses

Local regulations ensure horses are well-cared for, with limits on working hours, mandatory rest periods, shade, and regular veterinary care.

🌅 Part of the Experience

A sunset cidomo ride along the beach is pure magic. It's not just transport — it's a memory you'll keep forever.

Underwater Paradise

Diving & Snorkeling the Gili Islands

The Gili Islands are consistently rated among the best dive destinations in Southeast Asia. With warm, clear waters averaging 28°C, incredible biodiversity, and dive sites for all skill levels, it's easy to see why.

You don't even need to be a certified diver to experience the magic — the snorkeling here is world-class. Turtles, tropical fish, octopus, reef sharks, and stunning coral gardens are all accessible from the beach or a short swim.

Turtle Heaven

Exactly what it sounds like. Green sea turtles and hawksbills feeding on seagrass in shallow, crystal-clear water. Accessible for snorkelers.

The NEST Sculptures

48 life-sized human figures in a circle on the seabed. An incredible underwater art installation that's become a thriving artificial reef.

Meno Wall

A dramatic drop-off teeming with marine life. Turtles, bumphead parrotfish, Napoleon wrasse, and occasional reef sharks.

Bounty Wreck

A sunken pontoon boat now home to lionfish, moray eels, and schools of glassfish. Great for night dives.

Island Culture

The Spirit of the Gilis & Lombok

Discover the rich culture, traditions, and warm hospitality of the Sasak people.

The Gili Islands are part of Lombok, home to the Sasak people — one of Indonesia's most welcoming and culturally rich communities. While the islands themselves are small, they carry the traditions, cuisine, and spirit of greater Lombok and Indonesian culture.

From the daily call to prayer drifting across the water, to the traditional ceremonies still practiced in villages, to the incredible local cuisine — spending time here offers a genuine connection to Indonesian island life.

Your image: local food

🍛 Sasak Cuisine

Try Ayam Taliwang (spicy grilled chicken), Plecing Kangkung (water spinach with chili sambal), and fresh-caught seafood. Lombok's cuisine is bold, fresh, and unforgettable.

Your image: village/culture

🏘️ Traditional Villages

Visit Sasak villages on mainland Lombok to see traditional lumbung rice barns, weaving workshops, and centuries-old ways of life still practiced today.

Your image: mosque/culture

🕌 Islamic Heritage

Lombok is known as "Pulau Seribu Masjid" — the Island of a Thousand Mosques. The Sasak practice a unique blend of Islam with local traditions called Wetu Telu.

Your image: crafts/textiles

🧶 Traditional Crafts

Lombok is famous for ikat weaving, intricate pottery from Banyumulek, and beautiful bamboo crafts. Take home a piece of Sasak artistry as a meaningful souvenir.

Getting Here

How to Reach Gili Meno

Getting to paradise is easier than you think.

✈️

From Bali

Fast boat from Padang Bai or Serangan (2-2.5 hours) direct to Gili Meno

🚤

From Lombok

Public boat from Bangsal or Teluk Nara harbour (20-30 min)

🏝️

From Gili T / Gili Air

Island hopping boats run regularly between the three Gilis (10-15 min)

🛫

Lombok Airport

Fly to Lombok International (LOP), then taxi to Bangsal + boat (2 hours total)
