Why Gili Meno?

The quiet island — peaceful, romantic, and unforgettable

🌴

The Quiet Island

Gili Meno is the smallest and most peaceful of the three Gili Islands. No crowds, no noise — just pure island paradise. Perfect for couples, honeymooners, and anyone seeking tranquility.

🚲

No Cars, No Motorbikes

The Gili Islands are completely car-free and motorbike-free. The only transport is by foot, bicycle, or traditional cidomo (horse-drawn carriage). This keeps the islands peaceful, quiet, and eco-friendly.

💑

Honeymoon Paradise

Often called "Honeymoon Island," Gili Meno is the most romantic of the three Gili Islands. Beautiful beaches, incredible snorkeling, stunning sunsets, and intimate dining make it perfect for couples.

🐢

Best Snorkeling

Gili Meno has the best snorkeling in the Gili Islands, with crystal-clear water, vibrant coral reefs, and hundreds of sea turtles. The Turtle Sanctuary is just steps from Adeng-Adeng.

⏱️

Only 1.5 Hours from Bali

Take a fast boat from Padang Bai or Serangan in Bali and arrive at Gili Meno in just 1.5 hours. Multiple daily departures make it easy to visit for a day trip or longer stay.

🌅

Incredible Sunsets

Watch the sun sink into the ocean every evening from our beach bar. The west coast location means we get the best sunset views on the entire island.

Things To Do on Gili Meno

From snorkeling with turtles to island hopping — discover the best of Gili Meno

Snorkeling & Swimming

The best snorkeling in the Gili Islands. Swim with sea turtles, explore vibrant coral reefs, and discover the underwater world right from our beach. No boat needed — just walk into the water!

Scuba Diving

World-class dive sites including Turtle Heaven, Meno Wall, and the Bounty Wreck. PADI courses available for beginners and advanced divers. Warm, clear water year-round.

Horse & Carriage Tours

Experience traditional cidomo transport — horse-drawn carriages that are the only motorized transport on the island. Sunset rides are magical, and it's the most authentic way to explore Gili Meno.

Kayaking & SUP

Paddle through crystal-clear waters, explore hidden coves, and enjoy the peaceful surroundings. Kayaks and stand-up paddleboards available for rent.

Beach & Relaxation

White sand beaches, crystal-clear water, and pure relaxation. Gili Meno is the perfect place to unwind, read a book, and soak up the island vibes.

Island Walks & Cycling

Walk or cycle around the entire island in just 2-3 hours. Discover hidden beaches, local villages, and enjoy the peaceful, car-free environment.

Sunset Watching

Watch the sun sink into the ocean every evening from Adeng-Adeng beach bar. The west coast location means we get the best sunset views on the entire island.

Island Hopping

Visit Gili Trawangan (15 min boat) for nightlife and parties, or Gili Air (10 min boat) for cafes and yoga. Boats run every 30 minutes between the islands.

Cidomo — Traditional Horse-Drawn Carriages

The authentic way to explore Gili Meno

The Gili Islands are completely car-free and motorbike-free. The only form of motorized transport is the traditional cidomo — horse-drawn carriages that have been used for generations.

🌿 Eco-Friendly

Zero emissions, completely sustainable transport that keeps the islands clean and peaceful.

💚 Supports Local Economy

Cidomo drivers are local families who depend on tourism. Your ride directly supports the community.

🐴 Well-Cared-For Horses

Horses are regulated, receive regular vet care, have mandatory rest periods, and work in shaded conditions. Animal welfare is taken seriously.

🌅 Magical Sunset Rides

There's nothing quite like a cidomo ride around the island at sunset. It's one of the most authentic and memorable experiences on Gili Meno.

Taking a cidomo ride is part of the authentic island experience. It's how locals get around, and it's the best way to see the island while supporting sustainable tourism.

World-Class Diving on Gili Meno

Warm, clear water and incredible dive sites

Gili Meno offers some of the best diving in Indonesia. The water is warm (28°C average), crystal clear, and home to incredible marine life. Whether you're a beginner or advanced diver, there's something for everyone.

Turtle Heaven

Shallow (5-15m) — Perfect for Snorkelers

As the name suggests, this site is teeming with sea turtles. It's shallow enough for snorkelers, making it accessible to everyone. You'll see green sea turtles and hawksbill turtles feeding on the seagrass beds.

The NEST Underwater Sculptures

10-15m — Unique Art Installation

Created by renowned artist Jason deCaires Taylor, this underwater sculpture installation is both beautiful and functional — the sculptures act as artificial reefs, attracting marine life. A must-see for divers and snorkelers.

Meno Wall

15-40m — Advanced Diving

A dramatic drop-off wall covered in colorful soft corals, gorgonians, and sponges. You'll see reef sharks, big fish, and schools of tropical fish. Perfect for advanced divers seeking adventure.

Bounty Wreck

15-25m — Wreck Diving

An artificial wreck that's become a thriving ecosystem. Home to lionfish, moray eels, and countless reef fish. Excellent for night dives when the wreck comes alive with nocturnal marine life.

PADI Courses Available

Whether you're a complete beginner or looking to advance your skills, PADI-certified dive centers on Gili Meno offer courses from Open Water to Divemaster. The warm, clear water makes it an ideal learning environment.

Local Culture & Heritage

Discover the rich culture of Lombok and the Sasak people

The Sasak People

The Gili Islands are part of Lombok, home to the Sasak people — an indigenous ethnic group with a rich cultural heritage. While the Gilis are tourist destinations, the local culture is deeply rooted in Sasak traditions.

Cuisine

Lombok cuisine is distinct from Bali, with its own unique flavors:

  • Ayam Taliwang — Spicy grilled chicken, a Lombok specialty
  • Plecing Kangkung — Water spinach with spicy sambal
  • Fresh Seafood — Daily catches from local fishermen
  • Traditional Sasak Dishes — Available at local warungs

Traditional Villages

Take a day trip to Lombok to visit traditional Sasak villages, where you can see:

  • Traditional architecture — bamboo and thatch houses
  • Ikat weaving — intricate traditional textiles
  • Pottery making — handcrafted ceramics
  • Bamboo crafts — traditional handicrafts

Islamic Heritage

Lombok is predominantly Muslim, with a unique blend of Islamic and pre-Islamic traditions. The Wetu Telu tradition combines Islamic practices with ancient animist beliefs, creating a unique cultural identity.

Mount Rinjani

Visible from Gili Meno on clear days, Mount Rinjani is Indonesia's second-highest volcano and sacred to the Sasak people. Multi-day treks to the summit are popular with adventurous travelers.

How to Reach Gili Meno

Getting to the best beach bar and restaurant on Gili Meno

From Bali

Fast Boat from Padang Bai or Serangan

Only 1.5–2 hours direct to Gili Meno

Multiple daily departures make it easy to visit for a day trip or longer stay. Fast boats are comfortable, air-conditioned, and offer stunning views of the Lombok Strait.

  • Padang Bai → Gili Meno: 1.5–2 hours
  • Serangan → Gili Meno: 1.5–2 hours
  • Multiple operators, book in advance during peak season

From Lombok

Public Boat from Bangsal or Teluk Nara Harbour

20–30 minutes by public boat. Bangsal is the main public harbor, while Teluk Nara offers faster private boat options.

  • Bangsal → Gili Meno: 20–30 min (public boat)
  • Teluk Nara → Gili Meno: 15–20 min (private boat)
  • Taxi from Lombok Airport to Bangsal: 1.5 hours

From Other Gili Islands

Island Hopping Boats

Boats run every 30 minutes between the three Gili Islands, making island hopping easy and convenient.

  • Gili Trawangan → Gili Meno: 15 minutes
  • Gili Air → Gili Meno: 10 minutes
  • Regular scheduled departures

From Lombok Airport

Fly to Lombok International Airport (LOP)

Fly directly to Lombok, then take a taxi to Bangsal harbor (1.5 hours) and a boat to Gili Meno (20–30 minutes). Total journey time: approximately 2 hours from airport to island.

  • Lombok Airport → Bangsal: 1.5 hours by taxi
  • Bangsal → Gili Meno: 20–30 min by boat
  • Total: ~2 hours from airport

Tip: Book fast boats in advance during peak season (June–September, December–January). Public boats from Bangsal are cheaper but slower. For the fastest journey from Bali, choose a direct fast boat to Gili Meno.
